# Slow Mail - Risk Register

Status: pre-alpha. Last updated: 2026-10-20.

Likelihood and impact: Low / Medium / High, judged by the documentation agent, not measured. "Owner" is a role, not a person; the product owner assigns names. Status values: Open, Mitigated (control exists in code, not yet proven on a device or real tenant), Accepted (owner decision), Gate (blocks real-mailbox use until resolved).

Evidence pointers: `GC` = [GRAPH_CONTRACT.md](GRAPH_CONTRACT.md), `COMPAT` = [COMPATIBILITY.md](COMPATIBILITY.md), `D` = [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md), `ASM` = [ASSUMPTIONS.md](ASSUMPTIONS.md), `TM` = [THREAT_MODEL.md](THREAT_MODEL.md).
